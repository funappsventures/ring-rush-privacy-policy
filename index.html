Ring Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Ring Rush game (“the app”), made by Fun Apps Ventures (“we”), collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: we do not ask for your name, email or any account, and the app's own data stays on your device. Information about your device is collected by Google for ads and purchases, as described below.

Information stored on your device

To run the game, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Game progress: missions cleared, stars, best scores, daily streak and play statistics.
  • Settings, such as sound, vibration and colour-blind mode.
  • Which in-app items you own and which style and ball you have equipped.
  • A short log of recent game events (for example “mission completed”), used only on the device. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

We do not receive any of this data. If your phone's backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, so your progress can be restored on a new phone. This is controlled in your phone's settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (for example model, operating system and language),
  • how you interact with ads.

Ads may be personalised based on this information, depending on your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. Wherever you live, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones).

Learn how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases, such as Remove Ads and cosmetic items, are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which item was bought, so it can unlock it. Buying Remove Ads stops the app from loading ads. Google Play's handling of your payment is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing your score

If you choose to share a score, the app opens your phone's share menu with a score image and text. Nothing is shared unless you pick an app to share it with, and what happens next is governed by that app.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The app does not require an account.

Children

Ring Rush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting data

Data saved by the app stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your phone's settings, which deletes it. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies; you can manage it through your Google account and your phone's ad settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if new features are added. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
